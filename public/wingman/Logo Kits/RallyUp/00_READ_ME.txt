WHAT IS IN HERE

00_Brand_Sheet/            Colours, clear space, minimum sizes and misuse examples (PDF).
01_Vector_Masters/         SVG and PDF masters. Scale to any size without losing quality.
02_Print/                  CMYK vector PDFs, and 300 dpi transparent PNGs at 25, 50, 100 and 200 mm wide.
03_PNG_Transparent/        Every logo version as a transparent PNG in several widths (up to 4096 px).
04_Icons_and_Shapes/       Square, rounded and circle tiles on each background, as PNG and SVG.
05_Web_and_App/            Favicons, apple-touch icon, PWA icons, iOS and Android app icons, store icons, share images.
06_Social_and_Email/       Profile pictures, banners and covers, email signature, watermarks.

WHICH FILE SHOULD I USE?

Website or app, normal screens      PNG or SVG from 03_PNG_Transparent / 01_Vector_Masters
Light background                    versions marked for light backgrounds (full colour or black)
Dark or photo background            versions marked reversed, white or for dark backgrounds
Printer, signage, merchandise       PDF from 02_Print/PDF_CMYK_Vector (send the vector, not a PNG)
Vinyl cutting, stamps, engraving    a black or silhouette version as SVG or PDF
Word, PowerPoint, Google Docs       PNG at 1024 or 2048 px
Social profile                      06_Social_and_Email/Profile_Pictures (circle files are for preview, upload the square)
Website favicon                     05_Web_and_App/PWA_and_Apple (copy the files to your site root and paste head-snippet.html)

THINGS TO KNOW

1. The vector files (SVG, PDF) were traced from the supplied PNG artwork. They match it closely, but
they are not the original designer files. For very large print (billboards, vehicle wraps) ask the
original designer for the source vector.
2. CMYK colours are straight mathematical conversions from the screen colours. Colours can shift on
press. Ask your printer for a proof, and match special colours to a physical swatch book.
3. The supplied artwork is the symbol only, so there is no wordmark or horizontal lock-up in this
kit. If you supply the RallyUp wordmark or brand font, add it to the lock-ups.
4. Clear space and minimum sizes in the brand sheet are recommendations, not an existing brand rule.
5. App icons use the light tile (#FFFFFF). Swap in another tile from 04_Icons_and_Shapes if you
prefer.
6. The banners and share images are templates with the logo centred. Add taglines or photos in your
design tool.
